# Flair Creator Boarding Check

An internal scorecard Flair's social team uses to evaluate creator partnerships.

**Live page:** https://norafrommarketing.github.io/flair-creator-matrix/

## What it does
- **Scorecard:** scores a creator out of 100 across Segment Match, Brand & Trust Fit, Content Quality, Engagement (Instagram, benchmarked by follower tier), Risk & Safety and Flair's four strategic pillars, plus bonus points, automatic deny flags, considerations and notes.
- **Value Exchange:** a negotiation guide for flight credits and flights in exchange, by creator tier, with approval bands.
- **Exports:**
  - Print / Save PDF for the scorecard, reviewer notes and this creator's value exchange
  - Copy row and Download CSV for adding the creator to a tracking spreadsheet

## How to use
Open the live page, or download `index.html` and open it in any browser. It's a single self-contained file, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
